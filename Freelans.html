About Holland Machinery

Established Industrial Expertise

Holland Machinery has been a trusted name in the industrial machinery industry since 1962. Based in Miami, Florida, we have been providing quality new and used industrial machinery to customers worldwide for over 60 years.

We specialize in CNC machines, lathes, milling machines, presses, shears, welding equipment, and a wide range of other machinery for the metal and plastic industries. Our 20,000 square foot warehouse in Miami houses a large, constantly updated inventory ready for immediate delivery.

Our experienced staff is dedicated to helping you find the right machine for your specific application — backed by decades of industry knowledge, a global network of equipment contacts, and a commitment to honest, professional service.
